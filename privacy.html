Privacy Policy for Atelier Michelle

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Introduction

Atelier Michelle (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you visit ateliermichelle.com (the “Site”) and use our client-side booking forms to schedule wellness services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with our services and manage your appointments.

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): When you use our online booking form, we collect your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
  • Service & Health Information: If you include notes in your booking regarding preferences, allergies, or specific physical conditions relevant to our massages and body masks, we collect and store that information to ensure your safety.
  • Automated Information: We may collect standard technical information when you visit our Site, such as your browser type, device information, and IP address, to ensure the Site functions properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for business operations, including:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing your appointments.
  • Communicating with you regarding your bookings, cancellations, or schedule changes.
  • Ensuring your safety during treatments by reviewing any provided health or allergy disclosures.
  • Sending you administrative emails, such as policy updates or payment receipts.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

We take the security of your personal information seriously. Data submitted through our HTML booking forms is transmitted securely and stored in our controlled, secure administrative databases.

  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal data.
  • Access to your contact and health information is strictly limited to Atelier Michelle staff and practitioners who require it to perform your requested services.

4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We only share your data with trusted third-party service providers when it is absolutely necessary to run our business. These include:

  • Payment Processors: If you pay online or via an invoice, your payment details are processed directly by our secure, PCI-compliant third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, Square, PayPal). We do not store your full credit card information on our servers.
  • Operational Tools: We may use third-party booking management software or email service providers to handle appointment logistics and customer communications. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you. You may also request that we correct, update, or delete your personal information from our systems, provided it is not required to be kept for legal, tax, or regulatory purposes. To make a request, please contact us using the email provided below.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.” We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
